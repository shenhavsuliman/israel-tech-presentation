The Game-Changing "Selective PIP Algorithm"

Unlike traditional air defense systems that fire at every incoming radar track, Iron Dome evaluates if the predicted impact point (PIP) lies inside protected urban polygon grids. Rockets calculated to hit open dunes, agricultural fields, or the sea are deliberately ignored, preserving irreplaceable interceptor inventory.

The Stunner Interceptor: Dolphin-Nose Innovation

The Stunner missile features a revolutionary asymmetric "dolphin nose" radome housing two distinct, co-located sensors that render electronic jamming ineffective:

  • Active Radar Homeseeker (RF): Tracks targets in severe rain, fog, and dust clouds at long distance.
  • Imaging Infrared (IIR) Multi-Color Seeker: Identifies true target thermal signatures and rejects flare decoys or chaff.
  • Hit-to-Kill (HTK) Kinetic Impact: Completely eliminates target warheads without explosive blast fragmentation, neutralizing chemical/biological payloads.

Arrow 2 vs. Arrow 3: Strategic Differentiation

ARROW 2 (UPPER ATMOSPHERE)
  • Interception Regime: Stratosphere / Mesosphere (Altitude: 10–50 km).
  • Kill Mechanism: Directional blast fragmentation warhead (destroys incoming warhead during terminal descent).
  • Target Set: Medium-range ballistic missiles (Scud, Shahab-3).
ARROW 3 (EXO-ATMOSPHERIC / SPACE)
  • Interception Regime: Vacuum of Space (>100 km, above Kármán Line).
  • Kill Mechanism: Hit-to-Kill (HTK) Detachable Kinetic Kill Vehicle (KKV) with thrust vectoring.
  • Milestone: November 2023: First recorded combat space interception in human history.
